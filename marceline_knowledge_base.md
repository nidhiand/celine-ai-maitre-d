# Bistro Marceline: House Guide

## The basics
- Address: 412 Amsterdam Avenue at West 80th Street, New York, NY (fictional)
- Phone: the line the caller reached
- Neighborhood: Upper West Side. Nearest subway: 79th Street (1 train). About ten minutes' walk from the Museum of Natural History and from Riverside Park.
- Seats 48 in the dining room plus a 10-seat zinc bar. The bar is walk-in only.

## Rive Hospitality Group (fictional)
Bistro Marceline is the flagship of Rive Hospitality Group, which runs three restaurants in New York. Céline books only for Marceline. For the others, give the phone number.
- Café Rive, West Village: an all-day café and wine bar. Open every day including Mondays, 8:00 am to 11:00 pm. Walk-ins welcome; good for casual meals and smaller budgets. Phone: (212) 555-0142.
- Marceline Brooklyn, Carroll Gardens: a sister bistro with a garden patio and a similar menu. Open Wednesday to Monday, closed Tuesdays, dinner 5:30 pm to 10:00 pm. Phone: (718) 555-0177.

## Hours
- Monday: closed
- Tuesday to Thursday: dinner 5:30 pm to 10:00 pm (last seating 9:30 pm)
- Friday and Saturday: dinner 5:30 pm to 11:00 pm (last seating 10:30 pm)
- Sunday: dinner 5:00 pm to 9:30 pm (last seating 9:00 pm)
- Weekend brunch, Saturday and Sunday: 11:00 am to 3:00 pm (last seating 2:30 pm)

## Reservation policies
- Reservations accepted up to 30 days in advance.
- The phone line can book parties of 1 to 6. Parties of 7 or more go to the events team.
- Tables are held for 15 minutes past the reservation time.
- Cancellations or changes: please give at least 24 hours' notice. No fee is charged, but the team appreciates the notice.
- Private dining: the back room, Le Salon, seats up to 18. Handled by the events team.
- Dress code: none. "Come as you are, leave a little happier."
- Children are welcome. High chairs are available; there is no children's menu, but the kitchen will make a simple buttered pasta or a half portion of the steak frites.
- Dogs: welcome on the sidewalk terrace only (terrace open May to October, weather permitting).
- Corkage: $35 per bottle, two bottles maximum, not for wines on our list.
- Accessibility: the dining room and one restroom are step-free from the street entrance.

### Dietary notes
- Vegetarian mains: wild mushroom tart. Several vegetarian starters and sides.
- Vegan: the kitchen can prepare a vegan plate of seasonal vegetables and lentils with advance notice.
- Gluten: the steak frites, duck confit, and trout (without almonds) can be prepared gluten-free on request, but the kitchen is not gluten-free and cross-contact is possible.
- The kitchen handles nuts, shellfish, gluten, and dairy. We record every allergy and tell the kitchen, but we cannot guarantee any dish is free of an allergen.

- Trout amandine: Mâcon-Villages or Chablis

- Escargots or onion soup: Crémant or Chablis
- Tarte Tatin or cheese plate: Sauternes

## Frequently asked
- Can I sit at the bar? Yes, the bar is walk-in only and serves the full menu.
- Do you do birthdays? Yes. Mention it when booking and the team will add a candle to dessert.
- Is there parking? No dedicated parking; there are garages on West 79th and West 83rd Streets.
- Do you take walk-ins? Yes, for the bar and when tables are free, but reservations are recommended Friday and Saturday.
- Gift cards: available at the restaurant and by request; the team will follow up.
